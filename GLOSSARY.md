# Glossary

**Overlay**: a modal, full-screen layer-shell surface that takes exclusive keyboard focus and closes on Esc or on a click outside its surface (the launcher and the power menu). Only one Overlay is open at a time, tracked by `OverlayManager.current`. Each Overlay is opened by the Hyprland global shortcut `yonux:<name>`. _Not_ an Overlay: the lock screen (a session lock, not a layer surface) and notification toasts (non-modal).

**Bar**: the top panel shown on every screen.

**Bar widget**: one entry in the Bar (`modules/bar/widgets/`), identified by an id in `bar.json` (read by `BarLayout`) and resolved by `BarModel`. Built on `TrayItem`.

**Tray popup**: a non-modal popup anchored to a Bar widget, opened by clicking it. Distinct from an Overlay.

**Palette**: the colour scheme generated by matugen from the current Wallpaper. `Colors` (`theme/Colors.qml`) runs matugen and maps its output to colour roles (`active`, `fg`, `surface`…), exposed as `Theme.colors`. Not named `Palette` in code because QtQuick already has a `Palette` type.

**Settings window**: the floating `yonux settings` window (`Super+,`), driven by the `HyprSettings` schema. Changes apply immediately (sliders after 200 ms).

**Settings store**: a schema of settings backed by one hand-editable JSON file in `~/.config/yonux/` (`SettingsStore`). `ShellSettings` (`shell.json`) and `HyprSettings` (`hypr.json`) are settings stores; each row of the schema becomes a row in the Settings window. `bar.json` is _not_ a settings store: it has no schema, and `BarLayout` owns it.

**hypr.json**: the full file of Hyprland settings the Settings window controls (`~/.config/yonux/hypr.json`), seeded with defaults and editable by hand. `HyprSettings` turns it into `~/.config/yonux/hyprland.lua`, which Hyprland loads after its modules and before `user.lua`, and applies live with `hyprctl eval`. Never edit the generated Lua.

**Notification toast**: a non-modal popup for one incoming notification, stacked at the top right under the Bar (at most three). It closes on its own timeout, on click (running the default action) or on its close button; critical ones stay until closed. Every notification also lands in the history.

**Control center**: the Overlay docked to the right edge (`yonux:controlcenter`, `Super+N`, or the bell Bar widget) holding quick tiles, the volume slider and the notification history as cards grouped by app. The history persists to `~/.local/state/yonux/notifications.json`; cards restored from a previous session are _inactive_: the app that sent them is gone, so they have no actions.

**Do not disturb**: a Control center toggle that hides Notification toasts and silences the notification sound; notifications still reach the history, and critical ones and the shell's own (`yonux`) still show. Game mode turns it on and, on exit, restores what it was before. Persists across reboots in `~/.local/state/yonux/state.json`.

**Wallpaper picker**: the Overlay (`yonux:wallpaper`, `Super+W`, or the Wallpaper row in the Settings window) showing a grid of the images in the wallpaper folder, with the matugen controls that shape the Palette. Clicking an image applies it and keeps the picker open.
