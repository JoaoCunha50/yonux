# Glossary

**Overlay**: a modal, full-screen layer-shell surface that takes exclusive keyboard focus and closes on Esc or on a click outside its surface (the launcher and the power menu). Only one Overlay is open at a time, tracked by `OverlayManager.current`. Each Overlay is opened by the Hyprland global shortcut `yonux:<name>`. _Not_ an Overlay: the lock screen (a session lock, not a layer surface) and notification toasts (non-modal).

**Bar**: the top panel shown on every screen.

**Bar widget**: one entry in the Bar (`modules/bar/widgets/`), identified by an id in `bar.json` (read by `BarLayout`) and resolved by `BarModel`. Built on `TrayItem`.

**Tray popup**: a non-modal popup anchored to a Bar widget, opened by clicking it. Distinct from an Overlay.

**Palette**: the colour scheme generated by matugen from the current Wallpaper and fed into `Theme`.

**Settings window**: the floating `yonux settings` window (`Super+,`), driven by the `HyprSettings` schema. Changes apply immediately (sliders after 200 ms).

**hypr.json**: the full file of Hyprland settings the Settings window controls (`~/.config/yonux/hypr.json`), seeded with defaults and editable by hand. `HyprSettings` turns it into `~/.config/yonux/hyprland.lua`, which Hyprland loads after its modules and before `user.lua`, and applies live with `hyprctl eval`. Never edit the generated Lua.
